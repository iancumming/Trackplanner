Strength & Conditioning

Overview

Strength & Conditioning supports your running performance by improving power, stability, mobility, and injury resilience. Use this page to track sessions, view recommended exercises, and build weekly structure.

Key Areas

  • Lower Body Strength (squats, lunges, deadlifts)
  • Core Stability (planks, rotations, bracing)
  • Hip Mobility (openers, band work)
  • Plyometrics (bounds, hops, jumps)
  • Posterior Chain (hamstrings, glutes, back)

Weekly Structure

Suggested weekly layout:

  • 2× Strength Sessions
  • 1× Mobility Session
  • 1× Plyometric Session
  • Optional: Conditioning Circuits

Level Guide

  • Foundation — FOR ATHLETES NEW TO S&C OR DEVELOPING BASIC MOVEMENT SKILLS. FOCUS- Learning correct technique , control , stability , movement patterns. Exercises use mainly bodyweight or light resistance.
  • Intermediate — FOR ATHLETES WITH GOOD FOUNDATION SKILLS AND S&C EXPERIENCE. Focus- Increasing strength and power while introducing greater resistance and more demanding exercises.
  • Advanced — FOR EXPERIENCED ATHETES WITH EXCELLENT TECHNIQUE AND MOVMENT CONTROL. Focus- Developing maximum strength , elasiticity and athletic performance . Exercises are more complex and require greater control and training experience.
